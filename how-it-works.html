How MaxxListing Works

Five stages, from choosing a program to closing day. The process is the same in every program — what changes is how much of the work MaxxCity Realty LLC does for you.

Stage 1

Choose your program: the price is known before you sign

Pick the service level that fits you. The seller-directed programs — Essentials ($450 flat, one time) and Plus ($600 plus $400 at closing) — put you in the lead with the brokerage's platform and support behind you. The broker-represented programs — Professional (1.0%) and Signature (1.5%) — put MaxxCity Realty LLC in the field for you.

Your price is in the listing agreement up front, with no long-term contract — and you decide what, if anything, to offer a buyer's agent.

Stage 2

Prepare & price: the listing form and the strategy

You complete your listing details through a secure portal — property information, photos, HOA and disclosure documents, inspection reports — and the brokerage is notified the moment you update anything, so nothing you submit sits in a queue.

Pricing help follows your program: Professional brings market comparables and pricing guidance; Signature brings a formal Comparative Market Analysis (CMA) with a pricing strategy. Professional coordinates professional photography (you cover the cost); Signature includes photography, video, and drone footage.

Stage 3

Listing live on the MLS: one entry, every major site

Your listing enters the MLS as a professional listing and syndicates to Zillow, Realtor.com, Homes.com, and the other major home-search sites — plus a featured listing on MaxxCityHomes.com. For seller-directed programs, listings go live within 24–48 hours of your completed form and payment.

That is the flat fee at work: the same MLS entry and site exposure percentage listings get, for a fixed price instead of a slice of your equity.

Stage 4

Showings & offers: the work behind the sign

In the seller-directed programs you handle your showings while the brokerage coordinates your documents and forms — and with Plus, every purchase contract gets an unlimited review before you respond. In the broker-represented programs the brokerage coordinates showings, markets the listing actively, and provides negotiation assistance on offers.

When offers arrive, what you offer a buyer's agent is still the choice you made in Stage 1. Offers are weighed on price, terms, and buyer strength — and in the broker-represented programs, negotiated by a brokerage that works to close.

Stage 5

Contract to closing: coordination until the keys change hands

An accepted offer starts the final leg: inspections, appraisals, lender and title requirements, paperwork in between. Plus includes closing coordination; Professional brings full transaction coordination with all parties; Signature assigns a dedicated closing coordinator and complete transaction management.

At closing, your fee settles exactly as agreed — a flat number or a known percentage, never a surprise.
